Senior Backend Engineer — Stripe
San Francisco, CA (Hybrid — 3 days in office)

About Stripe:
Stripe is a financial infrastructure platform for businesses. Millions of companies — from the world's largest enterprises to the most ambitious startups — use Stripe to accept payments, grow their revenue, and accelerate new business opportunities.

About the role:
We're looking for a Senior Backend Engineer to join our Payments Platform team. You'll work on the core payment processing infrastructure that handles billions of dollars in transactions. This is a high-impact role where your code directly affects Stripe's reliability and performance.

What you'll do:
- Design and build highly available, low-latency payment processing services
- Work on distributed systems that process millions of transactions per day
- Improve system reliability and observability across the payments stack
- Collaborate with product and infrastructure teams to ship new payment methods
- Participate in on-call rotation and incident response

What we look for:
- 4+ years of experience in backend software engineering
- Strong proficiency in at least one of: Python, Ruby, Java, or Go
- Experience designing and operating distributed systems at scale
- Familiarity with relational databases (PostgreSQL, MySQL) and caching layers (Redis, Memcached)
- Experience with event-driven architectures and message queues (Kafka, RabbitMQ, SQS)
- Strong understanding of API design and RESTful services
- Experience with CI/CD pipelines and infrastructure as code
- Excellent problem-solving skills and attention to reliability

Nice to have:
- Experience in fintech or payment processing
- Knowledge of Kubernetes and container orchestration
- Experience with observability tools (Datadog, Prometheus, Grafana)
- Contributions to open-source projects
- Experience mentoring other engineers

Compensation:
- Salary range: $180,000 - $250,000 depending on experience
- Equity, benefits, and annual bonus

Stripe is an equal opportunity employer.
